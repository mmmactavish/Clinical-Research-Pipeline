# Common Limitations Catalog

Used in Phase 4 to automatically suggest possible limitations. Match relevant items based on study type and characteristics.

---

## 1. Internal Validity Threats

### 1.1 Selection Bias
- [ ] Non-random allocation (inherent limitation of observational studies)
- [ ] Healthy volunteer bias (enrollees healthier than general population)
- [ ] Berkson's bias (hospital-based case-control studies)
- [ ] Self-selection bias (survey/volunteer-based studies)
- [ ] Attrition bias (dropout rate >20% or dropout related to outcome)

### 1.2 Information Bias
- [ ] Recall bias (especially retrospective exposure data collection)
- [ ] Measurement bias (inconsistent standards across centers/operators)
- [ ] Reporting bias (subjective outcomes affected by blinding status)
- [ ] Detection bias (knowledge of exposure status influencing disease ascertainment)
- [ ] Hawthorne effect (being observed changes participant behavior)

### 1.3 Confounding
- [ ] Residual confounding (unmeasured or imprecisely measured confounders)
- [ ] Confounding by indication (treated patients inherently sicker than untreated)
- [ ] Time-related bias (immortal time bias, lead time bias)
- [ ] Channeling bias (inappropriate comparator selection)

## 2. External Validity Threats

- [ ] Population representativeness (single region/ethnicity/age group)
- [ ] Strict inclusion/exclusion criteria → results not applicable to real-world
- [ ] Tertiary hospital population ≠ community/primary care population
- [ ] Volunteer effect → enrollee adherence higher than real-world
- [ ] Temporal limitations (standard of care has since changed)
- [ ] Setting limitations (developed countries vs. resource-limited settings)

## 3. Statistical Limitations

- [ ] Insufficient sample size (especially for subgroup analyses)
- [ ] Multiple comparisons (uncorrected multiplicity)
- [ ] Insufficient power (Type II error risk)
- [ ] Missing data (analysis assumptions may not hold)
- [ ] Surrogate endpoint ≠ hard endpoint
- [ ] Subjectivity of non-inferiority/equivalence margins
- [ ] Interim analysis not pre-planned
- [ ] Statistical model assumptions not verified

## 4. Implementation Limitations

- [ ] Intervention feasibility (cost, equipment, training requirements)
- [ ] Adherence issues
- [ ] Crossover/contamination (control group received intervention)
- [ ] Follow-up duration insufficient to capture long-term outcomes
- [ ] Data quality (inherent limitations of electronic health records)

## 5. Ethical & Regulatory

- [ ] Ethical concerns with placebo control (when standard treatment exists)
- [ ] Completeness of informed consent
- [ ] Vulnerable population protection (children, pregnant women, cognitive impairment)

---

## Usage

In Phase 4, automatically match 6-10 most relevant limitations from this catalog based on Phase 3 study design characteristics. Then confirm and supplement with the user. Do NOT mechanically list all items.
